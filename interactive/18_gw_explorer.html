About the data

Data: LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA Gravitational-Wave Transient Catalog (GWTC-1 to GWTC-5.0), downloaded from the Gravitational Wave Open Science Center, gwosc.org. Charts made with Plotly.

  • Masses, distances and spins are best estimates. Each has a 90% range (shown when you click a dot): there is a 90% chance the true value lies inside it.
  • Collisions are labelled using a rough 3 M☉ line between neutron stars and black holes. Objects near the line could be either.
  • 109 of the 391 collisions have no masses published yet, so they appear only in "Detections so far".
  • Distance is the luminosity distance in megaparsecs (1 Mpc ≈ 3.26 million light-years). Spin, χeff, measures how much the objects spin in the same direction as their orbit (positive) or against it (negative).
  • The detectors hear heavy and nearby collisions most easily, so the counts are not a census of the universe.
This research has made use of data or software obtained from the Gravitational Wave Open Science Center (gwosc.org), a service of the LIGO Scientific Collaboration, the Virgo Collaboration, and KAGRA.
